# Cohors · Diario

**The building diary of Cohors**, a mobile app to track your workouts and share your training diary with the people you train with.

Cohors is built in Italy by a two-person team, as a side project. The app's code is private: this repository tells how it's built.

## Where we are

- **Now:** the workout tracker works on our phones. We're building the social diary.
- **Next:** closed test on Google Play from March 2027, then the launch in Italy in spring 2027 (Android, in Italian).

## Support Cohors
