## Introduction
(field "Introduction", Markdown)

### Hi, we're Cohors 👋

We're a two-person team in Italy building **Cohors**, a mobile app to track your workouts and share your training diary with the people you train with.

Cohors is a side project: we build it in the evenings and on weekends, and we pay for it ourselves. No investors, no loans.

**What you'll find here**

The app's code is private, but the way we build it isn't. In [cohors-diario](https://github.com/Cohorsfit/cohors-diario) we publish:

- a **devlog** every two weeks: what we built, what broke, what we decided and why;
- the **public roadmap**;
- **release notes** for every version;
- the **thanks page** for everyone who supports us.

**Where we are**

The workout tracker is working on our phones: routines, supersets, unilateral exercises, a muscle map, weekly set targets and offline sync. Now we're building the social diary. The app launches on Google Play in Italy in spring 2027, Android first, in Italian.

**Why sponsor us**

Your sponsorship pays the costs of running the app, starting with the database that will hold every workout. Every dollar goes back into Cohors.

Thank you for being here. 🛡️
