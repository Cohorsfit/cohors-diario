# Roadmap

This is where Cohors is going. Dates are plans, not promises: we build Cohors in our free time, so things can move.

Last update: 21 November 2026.

## ✅ Done

- Exercise library with over 300 exercises in Italian, plus your own custom exercises
- Routines and programs, with supersets, tri-sets and giant sets
- Workout in progress, with previous values, rest timer and an interactive notification
- Unilateral exercises (left and right side on two rows)
- History, personal records and progress charts
- Muscle map with 16 muscle groups
- Weekly set targets per muscle, deload weeks and a weekly summary
- Body weight, measurements and private progress photos
- Offline sync: log your workout without signal, it syncs when you're back online
- Data export and account deletion

## 🔨 Now

- **Social diary:** follow people, a feed of workouts, comments and reactions, live stories taken during the workout
- Ranks: from Recluta to Legato, earned by training

## 🔜 Next

- Final design applied to the whole app
- **Closed test** on Google Play from March 2027
- **Launch** on Google Play in Italy, spring 2027 (Android, in Italian)

## 🗺️ Later

- Personal trainers who follow their clients in the app
- Nutritionists and diet tracking
- iPhone
- Other languages and countries
