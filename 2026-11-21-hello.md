# #1 · Hello, we're Cohors

*21 November 2026*

This is the first post of the Cohors devlog. Every two weeks we'll write here what we built, what broke and what we decided.

## What Cohors is

Cohors is a mobile app to track your workouts. You build your routines, log every set and see your progress: records, charts and a map of the muscles you trained.

It's also a **training diary you share**. You follow the people you train with, see their workouts and cheer them on. No random posts and no videos: only training.

The name comes from the *cohors*, a unit of the Roman legion. Ranks, words and colours come from there too.

## Who we are

We're two people in Italy. One builds the app, the other runs the social pages and the content. We work on Cohors in the evenings and on weekends, and we pay for it ourselves.

## Where we are

We started in October 2026. After about six weeks the workout tracker works on our phones:

- routines and programs, with supersets;
- a workout screen with the values from last time and a rest timer you can use from the lock screen;
- exercises done one side at a time, on two rows;
- a muscle map with 16 groups and weekly set targets;
- offline sync, because gyms often have no signal.

## What's next

Now we're building the **social diary**: followers, a feed, comments, reactions and live stories taken during the workout. Then the final design, a closed test in March 2027 and the launch on Google Play in Italy in spring 2027.

## One decision we took

The free version of Cohors has **no limits** on routines, exercises or history. Many workout apps limit these to push you to pay. We think the basics of training should be free.
